## There is no declaration page here

That is deliberate. Appendix 1 of the policy is headed *"Statements and
Declarations to be Signed by the Candidate and **Submitted loose with the
Thesis**"*. They go on the official **Research Degree Thesis Statements and
Declarations Form**, which you submit alongside the PDF (§2.7) — not typeset
into the thesis.

The form covers four things: which degree the thesis is submitted for; that the
work has not been submitted elsewhere; consent for deposit in the University's
Open Access repository; and a declaration that the work is your own and has not
been edited by a third party beyond what the Third-Party Editors procedure
permits (Appendix 2).

**Use the official form, not a copy of it.** Get the current version from your
PGR office or the student intranet — the wording is prescribed and revised with
the policy. Your final word count goes on it too (§5.5), not in the thesis.

You will sign a fresh copy of the same form for the final approved version after
examination (§13.8).

If you have seen older Cardiff theses with a bound-in declaration page: the
requirement changed. Follow the current policy.
